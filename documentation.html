Shiv Band & Light House

Project Documentation: Overview, Concept & Technology Stack

Krishna Nagar, East Delhi Est. 1996 • v0.1.0 • Delhi NCR

1. Executive Summary & Concept

Shiv Band & Light House is a specialized web platform designed for an established family-owned brass band and event lighting service operating in Krishna Nagar, East Delhi since 1996. The platform modernizes traditional wedding band booking by replacing fragmented third-party listings with an authentic, fast, and authoritative online destination.

Direct Customer Trust

Presents high-resolution real procession photos, verified client testimonials, clear service inclusions, and consistent business details (NAP: Name, Address, Phone) matched with Google Business Profile.

Frictionless Lead Generation

Enables instant contact via one-tap WhatsApp chat, click-to-call, and an interactive reservation form that captures venue, dates, and package requirements.

Hyper-Local Intent SEO

Targets high-intent Delhi NCR wedding queries with bilingual headings (Hindi/English), Schema.org structured data, and tailored wedding checklists.

Real-Time Owner Control

Provides a password-protected admin portal with custom cookieless analytics, inquiry tracking, review moderation, and Bing IndexNow instant indexing.

4. Core Platform Capabilities

  • Real-Time Review System: Customers can submit ratings, feedback, and photos. Includes Firestore-backed thumbs-up/thumbs-down reactions with optimistic UI updates.
  • Custom Privacy-Focused Analytics: Built-in tracker (src/lib/analytics.ts) logging session durations, referral paths, page views, and button clicks without third-party tracking scripts.
  • Interactive Admin Control Room: Full administrative view at /admin showcasing daily visitor graphs, conversion counts, review moderation, photo uploads, and an IndexNow broadcast button.
  • Educational Wedding Guides: Curated editorial guides (such as the Delhi Baraat Checklist) addressing sound ordinance restrictions (10 PM rule), vehicle permissions, and timeline planning.
  • Responsive Multi-Channel Communication: Direct WhatsApp intent links (wa.me) pre-configured with wedding inquiry messages alongside direct phone dialers.
